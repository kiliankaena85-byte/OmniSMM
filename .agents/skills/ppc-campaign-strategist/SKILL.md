# PPC Campaign Strategist (v2026.1)

## 1. Scope
Strategic framework for designing, executing, and scaling paid acquisition channels in high-velocity fintech, SaaS, and digital service platforms.

## 2. Unit Economics Engine
* **Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC)**:
  $$\text{CAC} = \frac{\text{Total PPC Ad Spend} + \text{Operational Costs}}{\text{Number of New Paying Customers}}$$
* **Payback Period**:
  $$\text{Payback Months} = \frac{\text{CAC}}{\text{Monthly Gross Margin Per User}}$$
* **Target ROAS (Return on Ad Spend)**:
  $$\text{ROAS} = \frac{\text{Attributed Lifetime Gross Revenue}}{\text{PPC Ad Spend}} \times 100\%$$

## 3. 3-Tier Budget Allocation Matrix
* **Tier 1: Search Capture (40-50%)**: Direct high-intent capture (B2B terms, brand conquesting, high-commercial search).
* **Tier 2: Retargeting & YAN (РСЯ) (30-35%)**: Re-engaging dropped registrations, abandoned checkout carts, and look-alike audiences.
* **Tier 3: Smart Campaigns / Master of Campaigns (15-25%)**: Algorithmic auto-targeting optimized on CPA (registration/deposit goal).
